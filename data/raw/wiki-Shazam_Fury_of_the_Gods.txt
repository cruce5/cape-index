'''''Shazam! Fury of the Gods''''' is a 2023 American [[superhero film]] based on the [[DC Comics|DC]] character [[Shazam (DC Comics)|Shazam]]. Produced by [[New Line Cinema]], [[DC Films]], and [[the Safran Company]], and distributed by [[Warner Bros. Pictures]], it is the sequel to ''[[Shazam! (film)|Shazam!]]'' (2019) and the 12th installment in the [[DC Extended Universe]] (DCEU). Directed by [[David F. Sandberg]] and written by [[Henry Gayden]] and [[Chris Morgan (filmmaker)|Chris Morgan]], it stars [[Zachary Levi]], [[Asher Angel]], [[Jack Dylan Grazer]], [[Rachel Zegler]], [[Adam Brody]], [[Ross Butler (actor)|Ross Butler]], [[D. J. Cotrona]], [[Grace Caroline Currey]], [[Meagan Good]], [[Lucy Liu]], [[Djimon Hounsou]], and [[Helen Mirren]]. The plot follows [[Billy Batson (DC Extended Universe)|Billy Batson / Shazam]] and his foster siblings as they fight the Daughters of [[Atlas (mythology)|Atlas]] to save the Earth.

A sequel to ''Shazam!'' began development shortly after its release in April 2019, with Gayden returning as writer, and Morgan joining soon after to revise Gayden's screenplay. Sandberg and Levi were also set to return by that December. The title and the rest of the returning cast were confirmed in August 2020, with Zegler, Mirren and Liu cast as the daughters of Atlas in early 2021. Filming began that May in [[Atlanta, Georgia]], and concluded in August.

''Shazam! Fury of the Gods'' premiered at the [[Fox Village Theatre]] in Los Angeles on March 14, 2023, and was released in the United States on March 17. Unlike its predecessor, the film received mixed reviews from critics, and was a [[box-office bomb]], grossing $134 million worldwide against a production budget of $110–125 million.

== Plot ==
<!-- Per WP:FILMPLOT, plot summaries for feature film articles should be 400-700 words. -->
Four years after [[Doctor Sivana|Thaddeus Sivana]]'s defeat,{{efn|As depicted in ''[[Shazam! (film)|Shazam!]]'' (2019)}} [[Hespera]] and [[Calypso (mythology)|Kalypso]], daughters of the [[Titans|Titan]] [[Atlas (mythology)|Atlas]], break into the [[Acropolis Museum]] in [[Athens]] to steal the [[Wizard Shazam|Wizard]]'s broken staff. Taking it to the Wizard, imprisoned in the Gods' Realm, they force him to repair it and reactivate its powers.

In [[Philadelphia]], [[Billy Batson (DC Extended Universe)|Billy Batson]] and his "[[Shazam Family|Shazamily]]" are drifting apart as they grow up and pursue their interests. One day, they rescue a group of people when the [[Benjamin Franklin Bridge]] suffers damage, but they are criticized for not being able to prevent it from collapsing, in addition to other recent collateral damage, being nicknamed "The Philly Fiascos" by the public.<ref>{{cite web | title=Shazam! Fury of the Gods movie review | url=https://www.rogerebert.com/reviews/shazam-fury-of-the-gods-movie-review-2023 }}</ref> Billy is worried about being kicked out of the Vasquez family home when he turns eighteen. In a dream, the Wizard warns him about the Daughters of Atlas, prompting the Shazamily to research them.

The Shazamily enter the [[Rock of Eternity]], where they encounter the sentient pen Steve. It writes about the three daughters of Atlas, and they realize that Anthea, Atlas's youngest daughter, is none other than a girl named Anne. She is someone [[Captain Marvel Jr.|Freddy Freeman]] is going to meet.

When Freddy meets Anne, he shows off his superhero self. Hespera and Kalypso arrive with the staff and steal his powers. Billy and the Shazamily try to save Freddy, but the daughters kidnap him, encasing the city within an indestructible dome and trapping everyone inside. He is subsequently imprisoned within the Wizard in the Gods' Realm.

The Shazamily asks Steve to help draft a letter to negotiate with Hespera for Freddy's release. Billy meets her at a local restaurant and, although the meeting is initially cordial, she and Kalypso soon fight the Shazamily. Pedro loses his powers during the battle, while Hespera is captured and taken to the Rock. Having planned her capture, Hespera easily breaks out and steals the [[Golden apple|Golden Apple]], the seed of the [[Tree of Life]].

Meanwhile, Freddy and the Wizard attempt to escape the Gods' Realm with the sympathetic Anthea's help, just as Hespera returns with the Apple. The Daughters argue as Hespera and Anthea want to use the Apple to revive their realm, while Kalypso wishes to plant it on Earth to destroy it. Freddy steals it but is soon discovered. Billy and the Shazamily show up, and Freddy reacquires his powers.

Billy and the Shazamily emerge with the Wizard at the Vasquez home, where they reveal their secret identities to their foster parents. Kalypso appears with the dragon [[Ladon (mythology)|Ladon]] to acquire the Apple and destroy the Vasquez house.

The Shazamily attempts to keep the Apple away from Kalypso, but everyone except Billy loses their powers in the skirmish. Kalypso retrieves it, using it to plant the Tree at [[Citizens Bank Park]]. It then spawns various monsters to attack the city.

Hespera and Anthea object to their sister's destructive plan, but Kalypso mortally wounds Hespera and de-powers Anthea. Billy, in despair, asks the Wizard to revoke his powers, but the Wizard assures him that he is a true hero and so worthy of them.

Heeding the Wizard's words, Billy flies off to stop Kalypso, while the Shazamily enlists the help of dark [[unicorn]]s to fend off the other monsters. Billy persuades a dying Hespera to help him stop Kalypso. Realizing the dome reacts violently to his lightning, Billy lures Kalypso to the park while Hespera shrinks the dome to contain them.

Billy sacrifices himself to stop Kalypso and Ladon by overloading the staff with electricity and destroying the Tree and Kalypso's army with it. Hespera acknowledges him as a true god before succumbing to her wounds, causing the dome to disappear.

Anthea takes Billy's grieving family to the Gods' Realm for his burial. [[Diana Prince (DC Extended Universe)|Diana Prince]], known as [[Wonder Woman]], appears and repairs the staff. Imbuing it with her power, she uses it to revive the Gods' Realm, restore Anthea's powers, and resurrect Billy. Billy, in turn, uses the staff to restore his siblings' powers. The Shazamily rebuild their home, while Anthea and Freddy start a relationship, and the Wizard takes up residence on Earth.

In a [[mid-credits scene]], [[Emilia Harcourt (DC Extended Universe)|Emilia Harcourt]] and [[John Economos (DC Extended Universe)|John Economos]] invite Billy into the [[Justice Society of America|Justice Society]] on behalf of [[Amanda Waller (DC Extended Universe)|Amanda Waller]], but both turn and walk away as Billy expected an offer from the [[Justice League of America|Justice League]], wishing be with his crush Wonder Woman, and is annoyed at how similar they sound. In a post-credits scene, a still-incarcerated Sivana encounters [[Mister Mind]] once again, enraged that he has not begun enacting their plan.

With the successful opening of ''[[Shazam! (film)|Shazam!]]'' in April 2019, [[Henry Gayden]] was revealed to be returning to write the script for a sequel. Director [[David F. Sandberg]] and producer [[Peter Safran]] were also expected to return.<ref name="GaydenReturn" /> According to Sandberg, the plot point of [[Fawcett City]], [[Philadelphia]] being trapped under a dome was inspired by ''[[The Simpsons Movie]]'' (2007).<ref name="Supes&BatsyCameosScrapped" /> Later that month, [[Michelle Borth]], who portrayed the adult superhero form of [[Mary Marvel|Mary Bromfield]] in the first film, said she signed a five-picture deal for the role and was expected to return for at least one sequel.<ref name="BorthReturn" /> [[Zachary Levi]] confirmed in June that he was returning to star as [[Billy Batson (DC Extended Universe)|Shazam]] in the sequel, and revealed that writing had begun ahead of a planned filming start in mid-2020.<ref name="LeviScript2020Filming" /> Sandberg and much of the first film's crew were confirmed to return in December 2019, when [[New Line Cinema]] and [[Warner Bros. Pictures]] scheduled the sequel for release on April 1, 2022.<ref name="April2022Date" /> This was delayed in April 2020 to November 4, 2022. due to the [[COVID-19 pandemic]].<ref name="Nov2022Date" /> In October 2020, the release date was once again shifted to June 2, 2023. Later release dates were December 16, 2022 and December 21, 2022. In August 2022, the final release date of March 17, 2023 was announced.

=== Pre-production ===
In June 2020, [[Marta Milans]] confirmed that she would reprise her role as foster mother Rosa Vásquez from the first film, and revealed that filming had been delayed by the pandemic.<ref name="Milans" /> The film's title was revealed to be ''Shazam! Fury of the Gods'' during the virtual [[DC FanDome]] event in August 2020, with returning cast members confirmed including [[Asher Angel]] as teenager [[Billy Batson (DC Extended Universe)|Billy Batson]] and Levi as his adult superhero counterpart, [[Jack Dylan Grazer]] as [[Captain Marvel Jr.|Frederick "Freddy" Freeman]] and [[Adam Brody]] as his adult counterpart, [[Faithe Herman]] as [[Darla Dudley]] and [[Meagan Good]] as her adult counterpart, [[Grace Fulton]] as [[Mary Marvel|Mary Bromfield]], [[Ian Chen (actor)|Ian Chen]] as [[Eugene Choi]] and [[Ross Butler (actor)|Ross Butler]] as his adult counterpart, and Jovan Armand as [[Marvel Family#Pedro Peña|Pedro Peña]] with [[D. J. Cotrona]] as his adult counterpart.<ref name="TitleCastAnnouncement" /><ref name="Cast2021Filming" /> The next month, Levi said filming would begin in early 2021.<ref name="Cast2021Filming" />

The film's release was delayed again in October 2020, to June 2, 2023.<ref name="Jun2023Date" /> The following month, [[Mark Strong]] said he was waiting to hear if he would be returning as [[Doctor Sivana|Dr. Thaddeus Sivana]] in the sequel.<ref name="StrongNov2020" /> In January 2021, Good said filming would begin that May,<ref name="GoodMay2021Filming" /> and [[Rachel Zegler]] was cast in an undisclosed "key role" the next month.<ref name="Zegler" /> Following her exhaustive filming schedule for Maria in ''[[West Side Story (2021 film)|West Side Story]]'' (2021), Zegler booked a meeting with the DCEU casting director Rich Delia, who was thinking about her for [[Supergirl (Kara Zor-El)|Kara Zor-El / Supergirl]] in ''[[The Flash (film)|The Flash]]'' (2023). Zegler lost out to [[Sasha Calle]], but Delia told her that he thought that she could audition for Anthea in the ''Shazam'' sequel. During the [[COVID-19 pandemic]], Zegler read on for the role through [[Zoom Video Communications|Zoom]] with Sandberg and Dylan Grazer, doing a chemistry test with the latter, who was instrumental in her casting due to Safran giving him the option to choose and Dylan Grazer chose Zegler out of admiration for her work in the ''[[West Side Story (1961 film)|West Side Story]]'' (1961) remake.<ref name="ZeglersAudition" /> Safran was producing the film through his company [[the Safran Company]], while [[Geoff Johns]] was also set to produce.<ref name="Zegler" /> Johns was ultimately credited as an executive producer.<ref name="JohnsEP" /> In March, [[Helen Mirren]] was cast as [[Hespera]], a daughter of [[Atlas (mythology)|Atlas]], with Zegler reported to be playing Hespera's sister,<ref name="Mirren2" /><ref name="Mirren" /> Anthea.<ref name="ZeglerAnthea" /> [[Lucy Liu]] was cast as [[Calypso (mythology)|Kalypso]], another daughter of Atlas, the next month.<ref name="Liu" /> Strong said in May that he would not appear in the sequel,<ref name="StrongNoReprise" /> and [[Cooper Andrews]] was confirmed to reprise his role as foster father Víctor Vásquez from the first film.<ref name="Andrews" />

[[Chris Morgan (filmmaker)|Chris Morgan]] contributed to the film's script.<ref name="SafranIGNJan2022" /> Early during the film's development, Sandberg wanted to bring back [[Mister Mind]], a villainous character teased in the [[Post-credits scene|mid-credits scene]] of the first film whom Sandberg voiced, for the sequel in a bigger role, but his inclusion was ultimately dropped due to it requiring too much of the film's runtime, aside that he had to drop several other elements to not "overstuff" the film.<ref name="NoMisterMind" /> In addition to bringing [[Gal Gadot]] back as [[Diana Prince (DC Extended Universe)|Diana Prince / Wonder Woman]], Sandberg initially planned to have [[cameo appearance]]s of [[Superman (DC Extended Universe)|Kal-El / Clark Kent / Superman]] and [[Bruce Wayne (DC Extended Universe)|Bruce Wayne / Batman]] by showing Superman unsuccessfully trying to break the dome while Batman just stands there and looks at Superman.<ref name="Supes&BatsyCameosScrapped" />

=== Filming ===

=== Post-production ===
In March 2022, Warner Bros. adjusted its release schedule due to the impacts of COVID-19 on the workload of visual effects vendors. ''[[The Flash (film)|The Flash]]'' and ''[[Aquaman and the Lost Kingdom]]'' were moved from 2022 to 2023 to allow time for their visual effects work to be completed, while ''Shazam! Fury of the Gods'' was moved up to ''Aquaman and the Lost Kingdom''{{'s}} previous release date of December 16, 2022, because it would be ready for release earlier.<ref name="Mar2022ReleaseChanges" /> In April, the film's release date was pushed back five days to December 21 to avoid competition with ''[[Avatar: The Way of Water]]''.<ref name="Avatar2PushBack" /> [[Rizwan Manji]] was revealed to be appearing as an unnamed character in July 2022. Manji was suggested for the role by Safran, who also produced the DCEU television series ''[[Peacemaker (TV series)|Peacemaker]]'' in which Manji portrayed Jamil. Sandberg approved the casting, despite the series being set in the same universe as the film, because he thought Manji only had a small role in ''Peacemaker''. He was surprised to learn that Manji has a significant role and appears in the opening title sequence of the series.<ref name="Manji" /> ''Peacemaker'' creator [[James Gunn]] suggested that Manji's character in ''Fury of the Gods'' could be Jamil's brother.<ref name="Manji2" /> In August, after [[WarnerMedia]] merged with [[Discovery, Inc.]] to form [[Warner Bros. Discovery]], the studio delayed the film to March 17, 2023 (again taking ''Aquaman and the Lost Kingdom''{{'s}} previous release date), to help spread out the marketing and distribution costs for its feature films.<ref name="Aug2022ReleaseChanges" /> This also moved ''Fury of the Gods'' away from ''Avatar: The Way of Water''.<ref name="Aug2022ReleaseChanges2" /> By the start of November, Sandberg confirmed that the final cut was complete and ready for release.<ref name="Completed" />

The final wave of the film's marketing campaign revealed [[Gal Gadot]]'s cameo appearance as [[Diana Prince (DC Extended Universe)|Diana Prince / Wonder Woman]], reprising her role from prior DCEU movies. Sandberg was upset by this reveal, as he intended the cameo to be a surprise. The film's [[mid-credits scene]] sees [[Jennifer Holland]] and [[Steve Agee]] reprise their DCEU roles as [[Emilia Harcourt]] and [[John Economos]]. This scene was originally the post-credits scene of ''Shazam!'' spin-off film ''[[Black Adam (film)|Black Adam]]'' (2022), and featured members of the [[Justice Society of America]] from that film, but ''Black Adam'' star [[Dwayne Johnson]] "vetoed" both moves, forcing Safran to bring in Holland and Agee. Strong and Sandberg reprise their roles in the film's post-credits scene.<ref name="Cameos" /><ref name="TheRock" />

The visual effects were made by [[DNEG]], [[Pixomondo]], Stereo D, [[Weta FX]], Territory Studio, [[Method Studios]], Static Chair Productions, RISE VFX, and [[Scanline VFX]].<ref>{{cite web |url = https://www.artofvfx.com/shazam-fury-of-the-gods/amp/ |website = Art of VFX |title = Shazam 2: Fury of the Gods (2023) |first = Vincent |last = Frei |date = January 27, 2023 |access-date = March 26, 2023 |archive-date = March 25, 2023 |archive-url = https://web.archive.org/web/20230325213057/https://www.artofvfx.com/shazam-fury-of-the-gods/amp/ |url-status = live }}</ref>

== Release ==
=== Theatrical ===
''Shazam! Fury of the Gods'' premiered at [[Fox Village Theatre]]<ref name="FixVillageTheater" /> in Hollywood on March 14, 2023, and was released by [[Warner Bros. Pictures]] in the United States on March 17, 2023.<ref name="Aug2022ReleaseChanges" />

It was originally set for release on April 1, 2022<ref name="April2022Date" /> before being delayed to November 4, 2022,<ref name="Nov2022Date" /> and then to June 2, 2023, due to the COVID-19 pandemic.<ref name="Jun2023Date" /> It was then moved up to December 16, 2022, as it was ready for release sooner than other DC films delayed by the pandemic. That date was previously given to ''Aquaman and the Lost Kingdom''.<ref name="Mar2022ReleaseChanges" /> The release date was pushed back five days to December 21 in April 2022 to avoid competing with [[20th Century Studios]]' ''[[Avatar: The Way of Water]]'',<ref name="Avatar2PushBack" /> before being moved to ''Aquaman and the Lost Kingdom''{{'s}} date again (March 2023) in August 2022 when Warner Bros. Discovery was trying to spread out marketing and distribution costs.<ref name="Aug2022ReleaseChanges" /> This meant [[IMAX]] screens occupied by ''Avatar'' would be available for ''Shazam!''.<ref name="Aug2022ReleaseChanges2" />
