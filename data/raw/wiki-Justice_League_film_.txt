'''''Justice League''''' is a 2017 American [[superhero film]] based on the [[Justice League|DC Comics superhero team]]. Produced by [[Warner Bros. Pictures]], [[DC Films]], [[RatPac-Dune Entertainment]],{{efn|name=RatPacReplacement}} [[Atlas Entertainment]], and [[Cruel and Unusual Films]], and distributed by Warner Bros., it is the fifth installment in the [[DC Extended Universe]] (DCEU).<!--It is not a sequel in the same sense as Avengers: Infinity War not being a sequel to Thor: Ragnarok even when there is a direct continuation--> Directed by <!--PER CREDITS, DO NOT CHANGE TO JOSS WHEDON; HE IS UNCREDITED, IN THIS CASE UNSOURCED OR ERROR-->[[Zack Snyder]],{{efn|name=director}}<!--PER CREDITS, DO NOT CHANGE TO JOSS WHEDON; HE IS UNCREDITED, IN THIS CASE UNSOURCED OR ERROR--> and written by [[Chris Terrio]] and [[Joss Whedon]], the film features an [[ensemble cast]] including [[Ben Affleck]], [[Henry Cavill]], [[Gal Gadot]], [[Ezra Miller]], [[Jason Momoa]], [[Ray Fisher (actor)|Ray Fisher]], [[Amy Adams]], [[Jeremy Irons]], [[Diane Lane]], [[Connie Nielsen]], and [[J. K. Simmons]]. In the film, following the events of ''[[Batman v Superman: Dawn of Justice]]'' (2016), [[Bruce Wayne (DC Extended Universe)|Batman]] and [[Diana Prince (DC Extended Universe)|Wonder Woman]] recruit [[Barry Allen (DC Extended Universe)|the Flash]], [[Arthur Curry (DC Extended Universe)|Aquaman]], and [[Victor Stone (DC Extended Universe)|Cyborg]] after the death of [[Superman (DC Extended Universe)|Superman]] to save the world from the catastrophic threat of [[Steppenwolf (DC Extended Universe)|Steppenwolf]] and his army of [[Parademon]]s.

Warner Bros. began to develop a live-action Justice League film in 2007 with [[George Miller (filmmaker)|George Miller]] set to direct, but the project was canceled due to production delays and budgetary concerns. [[Principal photography]] took place from April to October 2016. After Snyder stepped down from the film following the death of his daughter, Whedon was hired to oversee the remainder of post-production, including writing and directing additional scenes, as well as reshooting a large portion of the film, which changed many aspects of it; Snyder ultimately retained sole credit as director. With an estimated production budget of $300&nbsp;million, ''Justice League'' is one of the [[List of most expensive films|most expensive films ever made]].

''Justice League'' premiered at the [[Dolby Theatre]] in [[Hollywood, Los Angeles]] on November 13, 2017, and was released in the United States four days later. The film received mixed to negative reviews from critics,<!--Per WP:FILMLEAD, any summary statement "should reflect an overall consensus explicitly summarized by one or more reliable sources".-->  and grossed $661.3&nbsp;million worldwide against an assumed [[break-even]] point of $750&nbsp;million, becoming a [[box-office failure]]. A director's cut, titled ''[[Zack Snyder's Justice League]]'', was released on [[HBO Max]] in 2021, following a fan campaign that advocated for Snyder's vision for the film.

==Plot==
<!-- As WP:FILMPLOT requires, the plot summary should be kept between 400 and 700 words. -->
[[Steppenwolf (DC Extended Universe)|Steppenwolf]] and his legions of [[Parademon]]s attempt to take over the Earth using the combined energies of the three [[Mother Box]]es. The attempt is foiled by a unified alliance including the [[Olympian Gods (DC Comics)|Olympian Gods]], [[Amazons (DC Comics)|Amazons]], [[Atlantis (DC Comics)|Atlanteans]], humanity, and extraterrestrial beings.{{efn|Identified offscreen as the [[Green Lantern]] [[Yalan Gur]]<ref>{{Cite web |last=Anderson |first=Jenna |date=November 26, 2017 |title=Green Lantern In 'Justice League' Identified |url=https://comicbook.com/dc/news/justice-league-movie-green-lantern-yalan-gur/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20190414172725/https://comicbook.com/dc/amp/2017/11/26/justice-league-movie-green-lantern-yalan-gur/ |archive-date=April 14, 2019 |access-date=November 30, 2019 |website=[[ComicBook.com]]}}</ref>}} The Mother Boxes are separated and hidden in different locations. Thousands of years later, [[Superman (DC Extended Universe)|Superman]]'s death following the battle with [[Doomsday (DC Comics)|Doomsday]] reactivates the Boxes, attracting Steppenwolf again to Earth. He aims to gather the Boxes to form "The Unity", which would instantly devastate Earth and [[Terraforming|terraform]] it into a copy of his homeworld.

Steppenwolf retrieves one Mother Box from [[Themyscira (DC Comics)|Themyscira]], prompting Queen [[Hippolyta (DC Comics)|Hippolyta]] to warn her daughter [[Diana Prince (DC Extended Universe)|Diana]]. Diana joins [[Bruce Wayne (DC Extended Universe)|Bruce Wayne]] in an attempt to unite other [[metahumans]] to their cause: Wayne goes after [[Arthur Curry (DC Extended Universe)|Arthur Curry]] and [[Barry Allen (DC Extended Universe)|Barry Allen]], while Diana locates [[Victor Stone (DC Extended Universe)|Victor Stone]]. Bruce fails to persuade Arthur but finds Barry enthusiastic. Victor joins after his father [[Silas Stone|Silas]] and other [[S.T.A.R. Labs]] employees are kidnapped by Steppenwolf, who is seeking the Mother Box protected by humanity.

Steppenwolf attacks an [[Atlantis (DC Comics)|Atlantean]] outpost to retrieve the next Mother Box, forcing Arthur into action. Victor retrieves the last Mother Box for the group, revealing that his father used it to rebuild Victor's body after an accident almost killed him. Knowing this information, Bruce decides to use the Mother Box to resurrect Superman, not only to help them fight off Steppenwolf's invasion but also to restore hope to humanity.

[[Superman (DC Extended Universe)|Clark Kent]]'s body is exhumed and placed in the amniotic fluid of the genesis chamber in the Kryptonian scout ship, along with the Mother Box, successfully resurrecting him. However, his memories have not returned, and he attacks the group. Batman enacts his contingency plan: [[Lois Lane (DC Extended Universe)|Lois Lane]]. Superman calms down and leaves with her to his family home in [[Smallville (comics)|Smallville]], where his memories come back. In the turmoil, the last Mother Box is left unguarded, allowing Steppenwolf to retrieve it. Without Superman to aid them, the five heroes travel to a village in [[Russia]], where Steppenwolf's lair is located. They fight their way through the Parademons; just when all hope seems lost, Superman arrives and assists Victor in separating the Mother Boxes. The team defeats Steppenwolf, who is attacked by his own Parademons when they smell his fear.

After the battle, Bruce and Diana set up a base of operations for the team. Diana and Arthur resume their heroic duties; Barry acquires a job in [[Central City (DC Comics)|Central City]]'s police department; Victor continues to enhance his abilities with his father in S.T.A.R. Labs; Superman resumes his life as reporter Clark Kent and protector of Earth. In a [[post-credits scene]], [[Lex Luthor (DC Extended Universe)|Lex Luthor]] has escaped from [[Arkham Asylum]] and recruits [[Deathstroke|Slade Wilson]] to form a league of their own.

==Director's cut==
{{main|Zack Snyder's Justice League{{!}}''Zack Snyder's Justice League''}}

The divisive reaction towards the theatrical cut of the film, with Zack Snyder leaving directorial duties and the final cut of the film in the hands of Joss Whedon, has led to an argument comparing the situation to the one experienced by the film ''[[Superman II]]'' (1980). Both ''Justice League'' and ''Superman II'' feature a director who was replaced, for different reasons, before completion of a film, which led to a second director coming in and making substantial changes to the tone of each film. Although the reasoning behind each director's departure differs, [[Richard Donner]] was able to complete [[Superman II: The Richard Donner Cut|his ''Superman II'' cut]] in 2006. In the belief that Snyder had shot enough material for a finished film, a campaign for a "Snyder Cut" was started to allow Snyder to receive a similar treatment to that given to Donner. Arguments were made that Snyder's vision would be more cohesive to the previous films than the actual theatrical cut, which Snyder has refused to see. Warner Bros. initially remained silent regarding any intention of making a "[[Snyder Cut]]".<ref>{{Cite web |date=July 8, 2018 |title=Justice League: The Snyder Cut Movement Explained |url=https://screenrant.com/justice-league-release-zack-snyder-cut-movement-explained/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20181107104039/https://screenrant.com/justice-league-release-zack-snyder-cut-movement-explained/ |archive-date=November 7, 2018 |access-date=November 6, 2018 |website=[[Screen Rant]] |at=page 1}}</ref>

Simmons stated that the studio was working on the script of the sequel, alongside ''The Batman''.<ref>{{cite web |last=Fitch |first=Adam |date=October 21, 2017 |title=Justice League 2 Script Is Currently Being Written |url=http://www.cbr.com/justice-league-2-written/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20171022061508/http://www.cbr.com/justice-league-2-written/ |archive-date=October 22, 2017 |access-date=October 21, 2017 |publisher=[[Comic Book Resources]]}}</ref> Shortly after the release of ''Justice League'', Henry Cavill said that he was under contract with Warner Bros. to play Superman for one more film.<ref>{{cite web |last=Zemler |first=Emily |title=Henry Cavill on the secrets of Superman's return in 'Justice League' |url=https://www.latimes.com/entertainment/movies/la-et-mn-superman-justice-league-henry-cavill-20171119-story.html |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20171120194329/http://www.latimes.com/entertainment/movies/la-et-mn-superman-justice-league-henry-cavill-20171119-story.html |archive-date=November 20, 2017 |access-date=November 20, 2017 |website=Los Angeles Times|date=November 19, 2017}}</ref> In December, it was revealed that there were "no immediate plans" for Zack Snyder to direct any future DC films, being relegated to an executive producer position instead. This came after a reshuffling of film production staff at Warner Bros. due to the film's mixed critical reception and disappointing financial performance.<ref name="businsi">{{Cite web |last=Guerrasio |first=Jason |title='Justice League' is reportedly the last DC Comics movie Zack Snyder will direct |url=http://www.businessinsider.com/justice-league-reportedly-last-dc-comics-movie-zack-snyder-will-direct-2017-12 |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20180617165117/http://www.businessinsider.com/justice-league-reportedly-last-dc-comics-movie-zack-snyder-will-direct-2017-12 |archive-date=June 17, 2018 |access-date=December 12, 2017 |website=Business Insider}}</ref> By 2019, Warner Bros. had prioritized solo films over the project.
