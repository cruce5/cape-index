'''''Spider-Man: Brand New Day''''' is a 2026 American [[superhero film]] based on the [[Marvel Comics]] character [[Spider-Man]]. Produced by [[Columbia Pictures]], [[Marvel Studios]], and [[Pascal Pictures]], and distributed by [[Sony Pictures Releasing]], it is [[List of Marvel Cinematic Universe films|the 38th film]] in the [[Marvel Cinematic Universe]] (MCU) and the fourth film in the [[Spider-Man (2017 film series)|MCU ''Spider-Man'' film series]] following ''[[Spider-Man: No Way Home]]'' (2021). The film was directed by [[Destin Daniel Cretton]], written by [[Chris McKenna (writer)|Chris McKenna]] and [[Erik Sommers]], and stars [[Tom Holland]] as [[Peter Parker (Marvel Cinematic Universe)|Peter Parker / Spider-Man]] alongside [[Zendaya]], [[Sadie Sink]], [[Jacob Batalon]], [[Jon Bernthal]], [[Florence Pugh]], [[Tramell Tillman]], [[Marisa Tomei]], and [[Mark Ruffalo]]. In the film, Parker is on his own and dedicated to protecting New York City as the hero Spider-Man. His personal struggles and overworking cause his superpowers to evolve while he faces a new [[telepath]]ic threat.

Sony was developing a fourth MCU ''Spider-Man'' film by August 2019, and producer [[Amy Pascal]] revealed in November 2021 that it was intended to be the first in a new trilogy starring Holland. Work on the story began that December, with Holland more involved in development than he was for the previous films. McKenna and Sommers returned as writers by February 2023, Cretton was hired to direct by October 2024, and the title—derived from the 2008 comic book storyline "[[Brand New Day (comics)|Brand New Day]]" that reset Spider-Man's status quo—was announced in March 2025. The producers wanted Holland's Parker to be a "proper Spider-Man" for the first time, on his own and fighting street-level crime. Cretton re-wrote the script with [[Justin Kuritzkes]]. Ahead of filming, Bernthal and Ruffalo were revealed to be reprising their MCU roles of [[Frank Castle (Marvel Cinematic Universe)|Frank Castle / Punisher]] and [[Bruce Banner (Marvel Cinematic Universe)|Bruce Banner / Hulk]]. Filming took place from August to December 2025, with location filming in [[Glasgow]], Scotland, and throughout England. Soundstage work occurred at [[Pinewood Studios]] in England.

''Spider-Man: Brand New Day'' premiered at the [[Dolby Theatre]] in [[Hollywood, Los Angeles]], on July 27, 2026, and was released in the United States on July 31 as part of [[Marvel Cinematic Universe: Phase Six|Phase Six]] of the MCU. It received largely positive reviews from critics, with praise for Holland's performance, the action sequences, and the more mature, character-driven story compared to the previous MCU ''Spider-Man'' films. The film has grossed $2.345&nbsp;billion worldwide and set multiple box office records. It became the [[Spider-Man in film#Box office|highest-grossing ''Spider-Man'' film]], the [[2026 in film#Highest-grossing films|highest-grossing film of 2026]], and the [[List of highest-grossing films|third highest-grossing film of all time]].

== Plot ==
<!-- Per WP:FILMPLOT, plot summaries for feature films should be between 400 and 700 words. Please check the word count before making any additions. -->
<!-- Per [[WP:MCUSURNAMES]], use surnames except where characters need to be differentiated. There is consensus to use first names for MJ and Ned. -->
After Dr. [[Stephen Strange (Marvel Cinematic Universe)|Stephen Strange]]'s magic spell makes the world forget that [[Peter Parker (Marvel Cinematic Universe)|Peter Parker]] exists,<!-- Notes are not necessary here because this information is within the same sub-franchise of the MCU. --> he chooses to protect his girlfriend [[MJ (Marvel Cinematic Universe)|Michelle "MJ" Jones-Watson]] and best friend [[Ned Leeds (Marvel Cinematic Universe)|Ned Leeds]] by not reintroducing himself to them. Over the next four years, Parker anonymously protects [[New York City]] as its celebrated hero Spider-Man. He defeats major criminals, including [[Mac Gargan (Marvel Cinematic Universe)|Mac Gargan]] and [[The Hand (Marvel Cinematic Universe)|the Hand]] ninjas, earning the trust of police detective [[Jean DeWolff (Marvel Cinematic Universe)|Jean DeWolff]]. Parker regularly visits his aunt [[May Parker (Marvel Cinematic Universe)|May]]'s grave.

Parker stops [[Frank Castle (Marvel Cinematic Universe)|Frank Castle]] from causing collateral damage in pursuit of a tank that crashes into a [[Department of Damage Control]] (DODC) prison. The driver is possessed by a [[telepath]] who [[Mind transfer (biological)|body hops]] from person to person to help Gargan escape, but is unable to enter Parker's mind. [[Yelena Belova (Marvel Cinematic Universe)|Yelena Belova]] informs Parker that multiple DODC facilities have been attacked by someone searching for "V-Max". DODC director [[Bill Metzger (Marvel Cinematic Universe)|Bill Metzger]], now using the Hand for security, denies knowledge of "V-Max" to Parker.

Ned and MJ move back to New York after graduating from [[MIT]]. Parker encounters them and sees MJ kiss her new boyfriend. Returning home in distress, he wakes up the next day with heightened senses and [[spinnerets]] in his wrists. When the telepath attacks another DODC facility with a possessed Gargan, Parker loses control of his new abilities and brutally beats Gargan, inadvertently injuring a police officer. After DeWolff tells him to regain control, Parker learns from [[Bruce Banner (Marvel Cinematic Universe)|Bruce Banner]], who has an inhibitor device that uses [[siRNA]] to suppress his super-powered [[alter ego]], the Hulk, and builds his own inhibitor. While he tests it, the telepath finds his apartment and learns about MJ from an unsent reintroduction letter he wrote to her. Parker races to get MJ to safety, but the telepath possesses and almost kills her. He rescues MJ and identifies the telepath as teenager [[Jean Grey (Marvel Cinematic Universe)|Jean Grey]].

Parker takes MJ to Castle's hideout for protection, where she helps complete his inhibitor and a universal inhibitor. Jean attacks the DODC's headquarters with a possessed Banner, whose inhibitor is removed, causing him to become the Hulk. While Parker fights the Hulk, Jean possesses Castle to open a vault hiding DODC agents. Parker calms the Hulk, then blocks Jean's powers with the universal inhibitor while Metzger tranquilizes her. Jean mentions "Sara" before losing consciousness; Metzger claims he remembers thinking of a colleague by that name while possessed by Jean. Parker returns to MJ, who has found the letter. He apologizes for not telling her and declares his love, but MJ does not remember him nor reciprocate his feelings. Despite this, she invites him to her and Ned's apartment, where a masked Parker bonds with them both. After MJ describes not remembering anything while possessed, Parker realizes Metzger lied. Parker contacts Yelena, who learns from redacted DODC files that [[Sara Grey (Marvel Cinematic Universe)|Sara]] is Jean's sister, another telepath kidnapped by the DODC.

Metzger experiments on Jean at a DODC facility on [[Roosevelt Island]] that Parker infiltrates. Jean deduces that "V-Max" is the weathered label on the air vent in her cell, the last thing Sara saw—and mentally communicated to Jean—before she died. Enraged, Jean creates a [[psionic]] field that extends beyond the facility, freezing everyone within it except for Parker, including MJ and Ned. Jean rebuffs Parker's apologies and attacks him using the Hand. He removes his inhibitor and embraces his new abilities, defeating the Hand and stopping Jean from killing Metzger. Parker lets her enter his mind, where she experiences a memory of May saying she loves him for who he is. Parker and Jean reconcile over their respective losses. He senses Castle shooting at Jean from a distance and shields her, sustaining a critical injury. Jean drops the psionic field.

Parker wakes up in a hospital with a remorseful Castle, who says Jean remotely kept him alive with her powers. Jean leaves the city, Metzger disappears, and the DODC comes under investigation. At May's grave, Parker vows to stop trying to do everything alone, later agreeing to let DeWolff handle a robbery while he recovers. == Production ==
=== Development ===
==== Interest and writing delay ====
[[File:Tom Holland by Gage Skidmore.jpg|thumb|upright=0.75|alt=Refer to caption|Star [[Tom Holland]] (pictured in July 2016) was more involved in ''Brand New Day''{{'s}} development than he was for the previous [[Spider-Man (2017 film series)|MCU ''Spider-Man'' films]].]]
[[Sony Pictures]] said in August 2019 that it would not be making future [[Spider-Man (2017 film series)|''Spider-Man'' films]] with [[Marvel Studios]] and its president, [[Kevin Feige]], after negotiations to update [[List of Marvel Cinematic Universe films#Spider-Man films|the production deal]] between the two companies failed. Sony co-produced ''[[Spider-Man: Homecoming]]'' (2017) and ''[[Spider-Man: Far From Home]]'' (2019) with Marvel Studios and [[Amy Pascal]],<ref name="NoDealAug2019Sony" /> and was reported to be developing two more films.<ref name="NoDealAug2019" /> Following a negative fan reaction, Sony and Marvel's parent company [[Disney]] returned to negotiations and announced a new agreement at the end of September, allowing Marvel Studios and Feige to produce ''[[Spider-Man: No Way Home]]'' (2021) with Pascal.<ref name="NewDealSep2019" /> In February 2021, [[Peter Parker (Marvel Cinematic Universe)|Peter Parker / Spider-Man]] star [[Tom Holland]] said ''No Way Home'' was the final film in his contract but he hoped to continue playing the character,<ref name="HollandFeb2021Collider" /><ref name="HollandFeb2021GQ" /> and later added that any further ''Spider-Man'' films would differ from the previous trilogy and feature a tonal change.<ref name="HollandOct2021" />

Around the release of ''No Way Home'' in December 2021, Holland said he was unsure if he should continue making ''Spider-Man'' films, feeling he would have done something wrong if he were still portraying the character in his thirties. He was interested in a film being made about [[Miles Morales|Miles Morales / Spider-Man]] instead,<ref name="HollandNov2021" /> and added that he would be saddened to leave the franchise but he had achieved what he wanted with the character.<ref name="HollandDec2021" /> Despite these comments, Pascal hoped to continue working with Holland on future ''Spider-Man'' films,<ref name="HollandNov2021" /> and said work on the first film in a new trilogy was about to begin.<ref name="PascalNov2021" /> Aaron Couch at ''[[The Hollywood Reporter]]'' said Sony did not yet have official plans for further MCU ''Spider-Man'' films but hoped to continue its collaboration with Marvel Studios.<ref name="THRNov2021" /> Pascal hoped the partnership between Sony and Disney would last forever, and Feige promised that it would not break down again as it did during ''No Way Home''{{'s}} development. He said Holland would return in another MCU film and confirmed that Marvel Studios had begun discussing the story for the next ''Spider-Man'' film with Pascal, Sony, and Disney.<ref name="FeigePascalDec2021" />

By April 2022, Sony was expecting director [[Jon Watts]] to return from the previous trilogy after he exited as director of Marvel Studios' [[The Fantastic Four: First Steps|''Fantastic Four'' film]]<!-- Do not unpipe. When Watts left, the film did not have that subtitle. --> (2025).<ref name="WattsFFExit" /> Sony Pictures CEO and chairman [[Tom Rothman]] hoped the "whole group" would return, including Watts, Holland, and [[Zendaya]]—Holland's then-girlfriend and later wife who portrays [[MJ (Marvel Cinematic Universe)|Michelle "MJ" Jones-Watson]].<ref name="RothmanMay2022" /> In February 2023, Feige said writers were working on a story with "big ideas",<ref name="FeigeFeb2023" /> but this was put on hold in May because of the [[2023 Writers Guild of America strike]]. Pascal said work would resume after the strike concluded.<ref name="PascalMay2023" /> Holland said he was in meetings about ideas and "ambitions" for the film,<ref name="HollandMay2026Empire" /> and these were also put on hold due to the strike.<ref name="HollandJun2023Variety" /> He was happy with the creative reasoning for why a fourth film should be made, but was apprehensive about the stigma surrounding fourth films. He felt they "hit a home run" with the first three films,<ref name="HollandJun2023THR" /> calling ''No Way Home'' the "perfect send-off",<ref name="HollandJun2026Playlist" /> and part of him wanted to exit the franchise on that high note and "pass the baton" to another actor.<ref name="HollandJun2023THR" /> Holland only wanted to return if they could "do justice to the character".<ref name="HollandJun2023Variety" />

==== Restarting active work ====
The writers' strike ended in September 2023,<ref name="WGAStrikeEnds" /> and Holland confirmed in November that discussions had resumed.<ref name="HollandNov2023" /> He had more input on the story than with the previous films because he was involved in the creative process much earlier.<ref name="HollandApr2024" /> Holland tried to understand fan sentiment on the internet because "we make these movies for [the fans]", and said fans were interested in a more relatable film where Spider-Man does not have to save the world.<ref name="HollandSep2025LADBible" /><ref name="HollandJun2026RT" /> He presented this "vibe" to the studios,<ref name="HollandSep2025Esquire" /> who also believed a more character-focused approach made sense after struggling to determine how they could go "bigger" than ''No Way Home''{{'s}} [[Multiverse (Marvel Cinematic Universe)|multiverse]]-based [[Crossover (fiction)|crossover]] story.<ref name="RothmanAug2026" /> Holland [[Pitch (filmmaking)|pitched]] that Parker's powers should go through puberty-like changes, which the creative team liked and developed further.<ref name="HollandMay2026Empire" /> Another proposal was to give him a pet dog, which was rejected.<ref name="DogScrappedIdea" />

Journalist [[Jeff Sneider]] reported in early 2024 that [[Justin Lin]] had been in contention to direct;<ref name="SneiderFilming" /> that [[Chris McKenna (writer)|Chris McKenna]] and [[Erik Sommers]] had returned from the prior three films to write the script;<ref name="SneiderMcKennaSommers" /> and that filming was set to begin in September or October of that year, delaying filming for the [[Euphoria season 3|third season]] of ''[[Euphoria (American TV series)|Euphoria]]'' (2026) to accommodate Zendaya's schedule.<ref name="SneiderFilming" /> In April, Holland said they were giving the writers the time they needed to work on the script following the strike.<ref name="HollandApr2024" /> Feige said in July that a script draft was being submitted "relatively soon",<ref name="FeigeJuly2024Draft" /> and Watts might not direct due to his schedule.<ref name="FeigeJuly2024Watts" /> Watts ultimately chose not to direct the film.<ref name="NoWatts" />

[[File:Destin Daniel Cretton by Gage Skidmore.jpg|thumb|upright=0.75|left|alt=Destin Daniel Cretton pictured in July 2019|[[Destin Daniel Cretton]] was hired to direct the film after working on other [[Marvel Studios]] projects.]]
After further work on the script, the studios began meeting with directors. [[Destin Daniel Cretton]] was the top choice to direct by September 2024.<ref name="CrettonTalksDeadline" /><ref name="CrettonTalksTHR" /> He directed the film ''[[Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings]]'' (2021) for Marvel Studios and signed an overall deal with them in December 2021 that included directing a sequel to that film and developing the [[Disney+]] series ''[[Wonder Man (TV series)|Wonder Man]]'' (2026). Cretton was also hired to direct the MCU crossover film ''[[Avengers: The Kang Dynasty]]'' in July 2022,<ref name="CrettonTKDVariety" /> but stepped down in November 2023;<ref name="CrettonTKDExitTHR" /> ''The Kang Dynasty'' was subsequently reworked into ''[[Avengers: Doomsday]]'' (2026).<ref name="SDCC2024Avengers" /> Cretton later said he did not feel like the right fit for that story.<ref name="CrettonAug2026Wrap" /> He discussed other potential projects with Feige and the fourth ''Spider-Man'' film was raised as a possibility.<ref name="CrettonJul2026IW" /> It was said to be the priority for Marvel Studios over a sequel to ''Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings''.<ref name="CrettonTalksVariety" /> Feige and Pascal informed Cretton of their plans for a more grounded story that focused on Parker's isolation following the events of ''No Way Home'', and he was interested in exploring the character's loneliness.<ref name="CrettonJul2026Credits" /> As Cretton entered talks to direct in September, Holland and Zendaya were also reportedly in talks to return, McKenna and Sommers were confirmed to be writing, and filming was expected to begin in early 2025.<ref name="CrettonTalksDeadline" /><ref name="CrettonTalksTHR" />

In October 2024, Holland was cast in ''[[The Odyssey (2026 film)|The Odyssey]]'' (2026),<ref name="HollandOct2024Schedule" /> and Sony approved his request to delay filming by six months so he could complete that film first.<ref name="HollandJun2026GoodHang" /><ref name="HollandKuritzkes" /> Cretton denied that he only joined the film because of this delay, but appreciated the extra development time.<ref name="CrettonJul2026IW" /> Holland was excited after reading a script draft with Zendaya, but he felt further work was needed,<ref name="HollandOct2024" /> and said the script was "going in a hundred different directions" before Cretton's hiring.<ref name="PlaylistJul2026" /> He confirmed that filming would now begin in mid-2025,<ref name="HollandOct2024Filming" /> and said it would be challenging but achievable to fit into the overall plan for the MCU.<ref name="HollandOct2024" /> At the end of October, Holland and Cretton were confirmed to have signed on and the film was scheduled for release on July 24, 2026.<ref name="Jul2026Date" /> [[Justin Kuritzkes]], who worked with Zendaya on the film ''[[Challengers (film)|Challengers]]'' (2024), rewrote the script with Cretton during the additional development period,<ref name="HollandJun2026GoodHang" /><ref name="HollandKuritzkes" /><ref name="KuritzkesWrap" /> after McKenna and Sommers left to work on ''Doomsday'' and ''[[Avengers: Secret Wars]]'' (2027). Cretton said Kuritzkes brought a different perspective and wrote "some of the most beautiful scenes", including the memory Parker has of his aunt [[May Parker (Marvel Cinematic Universe)|May]]. Kuritzkes continued working with Cretton until the film was finished,<ref name="CrettonAug2026Wrap" /> and received an offscreen "additional literary material" credit; McKenna and Sommers were officially credited as writers.<ref name="WGACredits" />

Throughout late 2024, Sneider reported on shifting production schedules and potential changes to Zendaya's role due to her commitments to ''Euphoria''{{'s}} third season, ''The Odyssey'', and the film ''[[Dune: Part Three]]'' (2026), which were all scheduled to be filmed in 2025.<ref name="Sneider2026Release" /><ref name="SneiderDec2024Updates" /> Katcy Stephan at ''[[Variety (magazine)|Variety]]'' also reported that Zendaya's role in the next ''Spider-Man'' film was unclear following her November 2024 casting in ''The Odyssey''.<ref name="ZendayaReturnUnclear" /> Sneider reported that [[Charlie Cox]] would reprise his role as [[Matt Murdock (Marvel Cinematic Universe)|Matt Murdock / Daredevil]] from ''No Way Home'' and other MCU media,<ref name="SneiderDec2024Updates" /> but Cox denied his involvement in October 2025.<ref name="CoxDenial" />

=== Pre-production ===
In February 2025, filming was confirmed to start in mid-2025 in London,<ref name="LondonFilming" /> and the release date was pushed back a week to July 31, 2026, to distance it from the release of ''The Odyssey'' earlier that month.<ref name="ZendayaReturnUnclear" /><ref name="Jul2026DateDelay" /><ref name="Jul2026DateDelay2" /> In March, [[Sadie Sink]] was cast as [[Jean Grey (Marvel Cinematic Universe)|Jean Grey]], a member of the [[X-Men]] in the comics.<ref name="SinkGrey" /><ref name="Sink" /> She was offered the role without auditioning,<ref name="SinkJul2026Nylon" /> having worked with Cretton on the film ''[[The Glass Castle (2017 film)|The Glass Castle]]'' (2017).<ref name="CrettonJul2026RS" /> Her character was kept secret until the film's release,<ref name="SinkGrey" /> and became the subject of speculation, with many believing it to be Jean.<ref name="SinkFanTheories" /> Pascal likened this to [[Tobey Maguire]] and [[Andrew Garfield]] appearing as previous versions of Spider-Man in ''No Way Home'', which was also kept secret until that film's release and became the subject of speculation.<ref name="PascalJul2026TF" /> Pascal and Cretton both said the secrecy was for the benefit of surprising fans upon the film's release.<ref name="CrettonJul2026RS" /><ref name="PascalJul2026TF" /> At the end of March, Holland and Cretton announced the title as ''Spider-Man: Brand New Day''.<ref name="BrandNewDay" /> Mckenna and Sommers said the creative team wanted to differentiate the film from the previous trilogy by not using "home" in the title. "[[Brand New Day (comics)|Brand New Day]]" is a 2008 comic book storyline that has some similarities to this film, and they found that title "just too appealing" for a story that shows a fresh start for its main character.<ref name="McKennaSommersAug2026CS" />

The filmmakers were in constant discussions with the creative team behind the Disney+ series ''[[Daredevil: Born Again]]'' (2025–present), to ensure "impacts are felt" between the two projects and events line up for their shared characters,<ref name="WinderbaumOct2025EW" /> such as Castle and [[Sheila Rivera]].<ref name="Bernthal" /><ref name="Guevara" /> [[Marvel Television (Marvel Studios label)|Marvel Television]] head [[Brad Winderbaum]] said the tones of each project were different but they exist in the same world.<ref name="WinderbaumOct2025EW" /> Following rumors that [[Mark Ruffalo]] would reprise his MCU role of [[Bruce Banner (Marvel Cinematic Universe)|Bruce Banner / Hulk]], his casting was confirmed with the start of filming in August 2025. ''The Hollywood Reporter''{{'s}} Couch and Borys Kit said Ruffalo's role only "firmed up" as the script came together in the lead-up to filming. The previous MCU ''Spider-Man'' films each featured a major MCU co-star as part of Sony's deal with Marvel Studios, similar to the ''[[Marvel Team-Up]]'' comic books that feature Spider-Man [[team-up|teaming-up]] with different heroes. They felt ''Brand New Day'' was "stacking the deck" by featuring both Bernthal and Ruffalo.<ref name="RuffaloMando" /><ref name="RuffaloMando2" /> Holland previously expressed interest in Spider-Man teaming-up with the Hulk, enjoying an image from the comics of the pair together and believing they should have a "big brother–little brother" dynamic.<ref name="HollandHulkInterest" /> Bringing the two characters together on screen was a long-held dream for Feige.<ref name="PascalJul2026CBR" /> Also in August, [[Michael Mando]] was set to reprise his role as [[Mac Gargan (Marvel Cinematic Universe)|Mac Gargan / Scorpion]] from ''Homecoming'', following up on that film's [[mid-credits scene]].<ref name="RuffaloMando" /><ref name="RuffaloMando2" />

==== Story ====
Holland and Cretton described ''Brand New Day'' as the start of a new chapter for Parker that would have a more mature tone than the previous trilogy.<ref name="HollandJun2026Playlist" /><ref name="HollandSep2025Complex" /><ref name="CrettonJan2026CB" /> Those films explored an expanded version of the [[Three-act structure|first act]] of [[Sam Raimi]]'s film ''[[Spider-Man (2002 film)|Spider-Man]]'' (2002)—which shows Maguire's [[Peter Parker (2002 film series character)|Peter Parker]] in high school—that concludes with Holland's Parker living on his own at the end of ''No Way Home''.<ref name="FeigePascalJul2026Collider" /> Feige said this was a promise that audiences would see him as a "proper Spider-Man" for the first time, with "classic elements" from the comics and Spider-Man fighting street-level crime rather than dealing with "world-ending events".<ref name="FeigeBNDJul2025" /><ref name="FeigeMay2026Empire" /> Marvel wanted to include other street-level heroes that Holland's Spider-Man had not interacted with before, such as the Punisher.<ref name="FeigeBNDJul2025" /> Unlike the previous trilogy, which used each film's major MCU co-star as a mentor for Parker, ''Brand New Day'' depicts him having more of a coworker relationship with the supporting characters. McKenna and Sommers said this was part of Parker growing up and no longer looking to more experienced heroes for guidance.<ref name="McKennaSommersAug2026Wrap" />

''No Way Home'' ends with Dr. [[Stephen Strange (Marvel Cinematic Universe)|Stephen Strange]] casting a spell to make the world forget that Parker exists. MJ and Ned remember their interactions with Spider-Man but not who he is.<ref name="HollandJun2026IGN" /> Cretton said there were many discussions about the mechanics of the spell, and they followed set rules such as not showing any pictures that Parker should appear in.<ref name="CrettonJul2026IW" /> ''Brand New Day'' is set four years after the spell was cast,<ref name="FeigePascalJul2026Collider" /> allowing the characters to grow up since ''No Way Home''.<ref name="DoGCovers" /> Holland said Parker is in "the darkest chapter of his life",<ref name="ParkerDarkestChapter" /> fully dedicating himself to being Spider-Man and neglecting his civilian life. He said Parker's inability to [[Work–life balance|balance professional and personal life]] was one of the film's core themes, and an important message for young people in the [[digital age]].<ref name="HollandJun2026Playlist" /> The character's trauma and stress trigger an evolution in his superpowers, including gaining the ability to shoot organic webs from his wrists similar to the 2005–06 comic book storyline "[[The Other (comic book storyline)|The Other]]". Cretton said this is frightening for Parker, who initially believes he is becoming a monster.<ref name="NewPowers" /><ref name="ArtBookReview" /> Sommers said Parker always struggles to balance his civilian life with being Spider-Man, and in this film he "has given up so much of his humanity that the spider side of him is starting to fill that vacuum". After trying to suppress the transformation, Parker learns to embrace both sides of himself and find balance. McKenna said the film's title applies to its end as much as its beginning, with Parker's [[character arc]] ending with him able to "actually have a brand new day and move forward".<ref name="McKennaSommersAug2026CS" />

McKenna and Sommers thought the shapeshifting villain [[Dmitri Smerdyakov (Marvel Cinematic Universe)|Chameleon]] was an appropriate [[List of Spider-Man enemies|classic Spider-Man antagonist]] to use for a "metamorphosis" story. They considered giving him the [[telepathic]] ability to [[Spirit possession|possess]] people instead of making him a physical shapeshifter, to differentiate him from the shapeshifting alien [[Skrulls (Marvel Cinematic Universe)|Skrulls]] seen in previous MCU projects. Chameleon was unable to be included after Sony used him in the film ''[[Kraven the Hunter (film)|Kraven the Hunter]]'' (2024), part of [[Sony's Spider-Man Universe]] (SSU), so other telepathic characters were considered as the main antagonist,<ref name="McKennaSommersAug2026Chameleon" /> including an obscure comic book character who was deemed unsatisfying for fans, and a character suggested by Holland that also could not be used.<ref name="FeigeVillainOptions" /> One of the characters considered was Jean Grey, a young telepath who has a similar journey of trauma and loss to Parker, with no mentor figures or community of support.<ref name="FeigeVillainOptions" /><ref name="CrettonAug2026Deadline" /> Cretton said Jean became the "perfect" option once Sink's casting was suggested, and denied that she was included as part of a mandate to set up Marvel Studios' [[X-Men (upcoming film)|untitled ''X-Men'' film]] (2028).<ref name="CrettonAug2026Wrap" /><ref name="FeigeVillainOptions" />

''Brand New Day'' takes place before ''Doomsday'', which was originally scheduled to be released first. Cretton said its delay to December 2026 allowed ''Brand New Day'' to stand alone and include elements that would not have been possible if it came after ''Doomsday''.<ref name="CrettonJul2026SR" /> Pascal said the Punisher and the Hulk were included because they face similar problems to Parker,<ref name="FeigePascalJul2026Collider" /> with McKenna adding that Banner was the perfect character to help Parker with his mutating powers. MCU character [[Yelena Belova (Marvel Cinematic Universe)|Yelena Belova / Black Widow]] was featured in the script from the first draft.<ref name="McKennaSommersAug2026Wrap" /> The creative team wanted Parker to be able to defeat the film's minor antagonists within a day or two.<ref name="ArtBookReview" /> They include [[Lonnie Lincoln (Marvel Cinematic Universe)|Tombstone]], [[Boomerang (Marvel Cinematic Universe)|Boomerang]], [[Tarantula (Marvel Cinematic Universe)|Tarantula]],<ref name="TrailerTFVillains" /> [[Ramrod (Marvel Cinematic Universe)|Ramrod]],<ref name="Ramrod" /> and [[The Hand (Marvel Cinematic Universe)|the Hand]] ninjas who were previously seen in [[Marvel's Netflix television series]].<ref name="TheHand" /> [[Trapster|Paste-Pot Pete]] was considered,<ref name="CrettonAug2026OutNow" /> while Parker uses [[Shocker (Marvel Cinematic Universe)|Shocker]]'s gauntlet from ''Homecoming'' to fight the Hulk.<ref name="ShockerGauntlet" /> Cretton said there were discussions about many other characters being added, including ''Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings'' protagonist [[Xu Shang-Chi]].<ref name="CrettonJul2026Polygon" />

==== Design ====
[[File:SMBND Peter Parker Apartment - Suit 3D Printer.jpg|thumb|left|alt=A 3D printer working on a piece of red and blue cloth|Sony and Little Caesars created a replica of Parker's apartment from ''Brand New Day'' as a fan experience, including his "Fabricator" which builds and repairs his Spider-Man suit.]]
Charles Wood returned as production designer from previous MCU media,<ref name="WoodAug2026" /> and costume designer Sanja Milkovic Hays also returned from ''No Way Home'' and other MCU films. She said the first conversations for ''Brand New Day'' were about Parker being on his own, with no access to [[Tony Stark (Marvel Cinematic Universe)|Tony Stark]]'s money and resources, so he had to build his own [[Spider-Man suit (Marvel Cinematic Universe)|Spider-Man suit]] which Cretton wanted to look more homemade than Holland's previous costumes.<ref name="HaysAug2026Credits" /> Parker also builds an [[artificial intelligence]] (AI) called [[E.V. (Marvel Cinematic Universe)|E.V.]],<ref name="ScriptPagesEW" /> voiced by [[Naomi Watts]] who worked with Holland on the film ''[[The Impossible (2012 film)|The Impossible]]'' (2012);<ref name="WattsEV" /> and a homemade "Fabricator", a combination of a [[sewing machine]] and a [[3D printing|3D printer]] that builds and repairs his suit.<ref name="ArtBookReview" /><ref name="ScriptPagesEW" />

Holland was involved in designing the new Spider-Man suit.<ref name="HaysAug2026Credits" /> It is similar to the suit that was introduced at the end of ''No Way Home'', which was made by Holland's Parker following his interactions with Maguire's and Garfield's Spider-Men in that film. The script for ''No Way Home'' explains that Parker took inspiration from the suits worn by those characters when designing his own.<ref name="CostumeSR" /> Holland liked the idea of Parker "trying to be his big brothers, that he looks up to them and sees little details on their suits that he thinks are really cool".<ref name="HollandSep2025Complex" /> He tried on costumes originally made for Maguire and Garfield,<ref name="HollandJul2026NJ" /> and researched which details fans liked most about those suits.<ref name="HollandJun2026Esquire" /> The previous films used visual effects to perfect the suit, but for ''Brand New Day'' the creative team chose not to do this so wrinkles and imperfections could be seen, including Holland's face moving underneath the mask. Holland wears the suit for around 80 percent of the film, much more than the previous films,<ref name="HaysAug2026Credits" /><ref name="HaysAug2026CB" /> and he said it was built completely differently from his previous costumes which made it comfier and more flexible.<ref name="HollandSep2025Complex" />

Jean Grey wears a hood to hide her identity, with green and yellow colors used as a reference to the comics.<ref name="HaysAug2026Credits" /> Influences on Castle's appearance include the cover of ''The Punisher War Journal'' #15 by [[Jim Lee]] and [[John Buscema]]'s design for the character.<ref name="ArtBookReview" /> His vest was made by a manufacturer of real military vests and then painted with the Punisher's skull symbol.<ref name="HaysAug2026Credits" /> The filmmakers considered using the Hulk's original gray look from the comics, dubbed "Gray Hulk", before deciding the audience should see the more familiar green "Savage Hulk". Ruffalo's facial features were de-emphasized in the design.<ref name="ArtBookReview" /> Scorpion's armor and the end of his mechanized tail were built by specialty costume company FBFX. The rest of his tail was created with visual effects.<ref name="HaysAug2026Credits" />

Wood did extensive research in New York City before recreating parts of it on the streets of [[Glasgow]], Scotland, and on sets at [[Pinewood Studios]] in [[Buckinghamshire]], England. Two or three city blocks of buildings and rooftops were built on a [[backlot]], including a rooftop with a large "Silver Cup" sign. Other sets included Parker's apartment, which has a view of [[Queensboro Bridge]] to show that "he had this view of the city he could never join"; MJ and Ned's apartment in [[Queens]]; Jean's cell, which was built inside a larger space that is revealed when the walls are raised; and Yelena's [[Public bathing|bathhouse]] "office", inspired by a [[Victorian era]] bathhouse in [[Manchester]].<ref name="ArtBookReview" /><ref name="WoodAug2026" /> For the tank seen early in the film, Wood requested several symbols be put on it including a devil and a panther. The devil symbol was compared to the logo for [[Mister Negative]]'s [[Inner Demons (comics)|Inner Demons]] gang from the comics.<ref name="NegativeSetPhotos" /><ref name="TankSymbols" />

=== Filming ===
[[Principal photography]] began on August 3, 2025, in Glasgow under the [[working title]] ''Blue Oasis'',<ref name="GlasgowFilmingStart" /><ref name="VarietyFilmingStart" /> with [[Brett Pawlak]] returning as cinematographer from many of Cretton's previous films.<ref name="CrettonAug2026Deadline" /> The film was shot in two aspect ratios, [[2.39:1]] for wide screens and [[IMAX#Digital|1.90:1 for digital IMAX]].<ref name="Trailer2Marvel" /> Holland was inspired by director [[Christopher Nolan]]'s efficient approach on ''The Odyssey'' and wanted to bring a similar work ethic to ''Brand New Day'', insisting that they have each day's filming planned ahead of time,<ref name="HollandKuritzkes" /><ref name="HollandJun2026GQ" /> and that practical stunts be performed when possible.<ref name="HollandJul2026TF" /> Peng Zhang coordinated the fight sequences, returning from ''Shang-Chi and the Legend of the Ten Rings''.<ref name="CrettonAug2026Wrap" /><ref name="CrettonJul2026Inverse" /> Some stunts were inspired by the ''[[Marvel's Spider-Man]]'' video game series,<ref name="CrettonInsomniacInspiration" /> including its finishing moves.<ref name="HollandInsomniacInspiration" /> The production team analyzed footage of [[Wingsuit flying|wingsuit flyers]] and [[parkour]] athletes for the scenes where Spider-Man swings on his webs.<ref name="CrettonJul2026Polygon" /> For the fight between Parker and the Hand, when the latter are under Jean's control, Zhang choreographed the ninjas in a bird wing-inspired formation, referencing Jean's comic book connection to the [[Phoenix Force]].<ref name="ZhangAug2026" /> Dance choreographer Nathan Kim designed the body movements for when Jean is [[body hopping]], which were not altered during post-production.<ref name="CrettonJul2026Inverse" /><ref name="EditorsAotC" />

Bernthal was set to film his scenes back-to-back with ''One Last Kill'', which finished filming in early August 2025.<ref name="BernthalDec2025" /> Production for ''Brand New Day'' was scheduled for around two weeks throughout [[Glasgow city centre]]—including [[Merchant City]], [[George Square]], and [[Trongate]]—for scenes set in New York,<ref name="GlasgowFilming" /><ref name="GeorgeTrongateNY" /> including the film's opening action sequence with the tank.<ref name="BBCLocations" /> Holland was excited to start principal photography with [[location shooting]] after production on ''No Way Home'' was restricted to soundstages due to the [[COVID-19 pandemic]].<ref name="HollandGlasgowFilming" /> He was glad fans could experience the start of filming with the cast and crew.<ref name="HollandDayOne" />

Filming took place at [[Brookwood Cemetery]] in [[Surrey]], England, on August 7, 2025,<ref name="SurreyFilming" /> for scenes of Parker visiting May's grave.<ref name="BrookwoodAuntMayGrave" /> These were some of the first non-stunt scenes filmed. After filming the cemetery shots they needed for the opening montage, Cretton asked Holland to do another take that could be used for the end of the film. Holland improvised a line about deciding not to go it alone anymore, which Cretton added to the film. He said this became their "north star", distilling the film to a core idea that they could work backward from.<ref name="CrettonAug2026Deadline" /> [[Tramell Tillman]] joined the cast at the end of August,<ref name="Tillman" /> as [[Bill Metzger (Marvel Cinematic Universe)|Bill Metzger]], the head of the [[Department of Damage Control]] (DODC).<ref name="TillmanMetzger" /> Soundstage work took place at Pinewood for most of September.<ref name="PinewoodFilming" /><ref name="HollandConcussionFilmingPause" /> For the scene where Parker is bonding with Ned, Holland asked to stop filming because it did not feel organic and he felt it needed to be reworked; filming was stopped for the day and the actors all contributed ideas before Cretton and Kuritzkes rewrote the scene.<ref name="HollandJun2026Playlist" /><ref name="HollandJun2026GoodHang" /><ref name="BatalonAug2026THR" />

Filming took place in London's [[Battersea Park]] between early and mid-September 2025.<ref name="BatterseaFilming" /><ref name="BatterseaFilming2" /> On September 19, Holland suffered a mild [[concussion]] while filming a stunt on the Pinewood set,<ref name="HollandConcussionFilmingPause" /><ref name="HollandConcussion" /> where filming was paused until September 29 while Holland recovered.<ref name="HollandConcussionFilmingPause" /> [[Marvin Jones III]] was revealed to be portraying Lonnie Lincoln / Tombstone after voicing a version of the character in Sony's animated film ''[[Spider-Man: Into the Spider-Verse]]'' (2018).<ref name="Jones" /> Filming took place at [[Fanum House]] in [[Basingstoke]], England, with one of Holland's stunt doubles.<ref name="BasingstokeFilming" /> Filming at Basingstoke continued in early October, with Holland joining the filming there,<ref name="BasingstokeFilming2" /> along with Mando.<ref name="MandoFilming" /> Later that month, Holland, Sink, and Ruffalo were seen filming at [[Senate House, London|Senate House]] in London,<ref name="SinkSetPhotos" /><ref name="RuffaloSetPhotos" /><ref name="SenateHouseFilming" /> which stood in for DODC headquarters.<ref name="BBCLocations" /> In early November, filming took place at [[Ravenscourt Park]] in London,<ref name="RavenscourtFilming" /> for scenes related to Jean's backstory.<ref name="BBCLocations" /> Throughout November, filming took place at [[HM Prison Dorchester]] in [[Dorchester, Dorset|Dorchester]], England,<ref name="DorchesterFilming" /><ref name="DorchesterFilming2" /> for the abandoned prison where Parker fights the Hand. Other locations in the film include [[County Hall, Hertford|Hertford County Hall]] in [[Hertfordshire]], standing in for [[Empire State University (Marvel Cinematic Universe)|Empire State University]]; and Turk's Shipyard at [[Chatham Historic Dockyard]] in [[Kent]] where Castle's safe house is located.<ref name="BBCLocations" /> Cretton announced on December 19, 2025, that filming had [[Wrap (filmmaking)|wrapped]].<ref name="CrettonFilmingWrap" /> ''Brand New Day'' is the first film to have sequences filmed specifically for the [[ScreenX]] format; manufacturer [[CJ CGV|CJ 4DPlex]] shot additional footage to show on ScreenX's side screens.<ref name="ScreenX" />

Cretton designed several shots that recreate comic book covers or images,<ref name="FeigeBNDJul2025" /> including Spider-Man swinging with a civilian from the character's debut comic ''[[Amazing Fantasy]]'' vol. 1 #15 (August 1962); Spider-Man fighting Tarantula from ''The Amazing Spider-Man'' vol. 1 #134 (July 1974); Spider-Man dodging Ramrod from ''The Amazing Spider-Man'' vol. 1 #221 (July 1981); Tombstone holding Spider-Man from the side of a building from ''[[The Spectacular Spider-Man]]'' vol. 1 #142 (May 1988); Spider-Man being attacked by Boomerang from ''The Amazing Spider-Man'' vol. 1 #345 (March 1991); Scorpion's tail striking Spider-Man from ''[[Spider-Man Adventures]]'' #2 (November 1994);<ref name="ComicCoversHomageMarvel" /> and Castle carrying an injured Spider-Man from ''[[Civil War (comics)|Civil War]]'' vol. 1 #5 (February 2006).<ref name="SREasterEggs" /> Many of these shots appear in the opening montage that shows what Parker has been doing since the end of ''No Way Home''. Cretton enjoyed imagining what happens before or after each of the comic images.<ref name="CrettonJul2026RS" /> Some of the shots were filmed at 420 [[frames per second]], and Cretton said it was "virtually impossible" for an actor to perform at that rate.<ref name="ComicCoversHomage" /><ref name="ComicCoversHomageGizmodo" /> A shot of Spider-Man and the Hand jumping at each other required at least 65 takes.<ref name="CrettonJul2026THR" /> In contrast, Holland achieved the desired pose for the Scorpion shot in two or three takes.<ref name="ComicCoversHomage" /><ref name="ComicCoversHomageGizmodo" />

=== Post-production ===
In January 2026, Justin Kroll from ''Deadline Hollywood'' reported that Feige had been solely focused on [[Production of Avengers: Doomsday and Avengers: Secret Wars|the production of ''Doomsday'' and ''Secret Wars'']] for the last year, and had let Pascal handle much of the production for ''Brand New Day'';<ref name="KrollFeigeSoleFocus" /> Cretton disputed this.<ref name="CrettonJul2026SR" /> In addition to Feige and Pascal, other producers on the film included frequent ''Spider-Man'' film producer [[Avi Arad]] and Pascal's producing partner Rachel O'Connor.<ref name="TrailerWrap" /><ref name="PascalAug2026Deadline" />

[[File:Florence Pugh 2025 (cropped).jpg|thumb|left|upright=0.75|alt=Florence Pugh pictured in April 2025|[[Florence Pugh]] reprised her MCU role of [[Yelena Belova (Marvel Cinematic Universe)|Yelena Belova / Black Widow]]. Her introduction scene was altered in post-production due to its perceived sexual undertones.]]
The first trailer was released in March, publicly revealing the Hand's return from the Marvel Netflix series;<ref name="TrailerTFVillains" /> [[Zabryna Guevara]] reprising her role as Sheila Rivera from ''Born Again'' for a shot in which her character hands Spider-Man [[Key to the city|the key]] to New York City;<ref name="Guevara" /> [[Eman Esfandi]] as a new love interest for MJ;<ref name="TrailerTF" /> and [[Keith David]] as the narrator of a [[nature documentary]] about spiders.<ref name="TrailerGizmodo" /><ref name="SREasterEggs" /> Feige indicated in mid-July that [[Florence Pugh]] would reprise her MCU role of Yelena Belova ahead of her appearance in ''Doomsday'',<ref name="FeigePughTease" /> which was confirmed at the end of the month,<ref name="Pugh" /> when [[Rosario Dawson]] revealed that she reprised her role as [[Claire Temple (Marvel Cinematic Universe)|Claire Temple]] from Marvel's Netflix series for a scene that had been cut.<ref name="Dawson" /> It showed her meeting Parker at a hospital after he fights Scorpion and before he seeks advice from Banner.<ref name="McKennaSommersAug2026Wrap" /> Additionally, [[Marisa Tomei]] reprised her role as Parker's Aunt May from the previous trilogy.<ref name="DeadlineCast" />

''Brand New Day'' was edited by [[Nat Sanders]], Gina Sansom, and [[Harry Yoon]], all reuniting with Cretton from previous projects. Sanders and Sansom began work during pre-production and filming, working with Cretton on the [[previsualization]] process, with Yoon joining later. Several rounds of test screenings were held during the editing process.<ref name="EditorsAotC" /> The first [[assembly cut]] was around 170 to 180 minutes, and was edited down to 144 minutes.<ref name="CrettonJul2026Collider" /> This was primarily done by reducing scenes, particularly action sequences, rather than cutting full scenes. Sanders and Sansom worked on the opening montage and tried to balance a fun opening with the sadness of Parker's situation.<ref name="EditorsAotC" /> In April 2026, [[additional photography]] was taking place in London after the second test screening,<ref name="EditorsAotC" /><ref name="HollandApr2026" /> adding new footage to clarify the connection between Parker's evolving abilities and his isolation, such as mentions of headaches and pain in his wrists early in the film.<ref name="EditorsAotC" /><ref name="HollandApr2026" /> Holland said they were putting "icing on the cake", adding humor and incorporating a villain plotline in a new way.<ref name="HollandApr2026" /> There was a cut that was influenced by the test screenings which he felt "totally didn't work",<ref name="HollandTestScreening" /> although Cretton said it was similar to the final version and had just been "overcut".<ref name="CrettonAug2026Wrap" /> Yelena's introduction, seemingly naked underwater in the bathhouse, was discussed for its perceived sexual undertones. The writers enjoyed how she was "putting [Parker] on his heels" in the scene, and the creative team felt it was appropriate for a more grown-up Spider-Man film.<ref name="McKennaSommersAug2026Wrap" /> Adjustments were made in post-production to make the water more opaque in response to concerns from younger test screen viewers who found the scene uncomfortable.<ref name="EditorsAotC" />

The film's version of Marvel Studios' opening logo explains the impact of Strange's spell on MJ and Ned's memories. It includes scenes from the previous MCU ''Spider-Man'' films where Parker is with those characters, and shows him disappearing from the footage.<ref name="ScriptPagesEW" /> This idea was suggested by Cretton in response to feedback from test audiences who were not familiar with the ending of ''No Way Home''. For the main-on-end credits, they wanted the audience to sit with the film's ending and Parker's character growth rather than jump to a "fun and inventive main title" sequence,<ref name="EditorsAotC" /> and Cretton wanted to reflect the themes of connection.<ref name="CrettonAug2026OutNow" /> The original suggestion was to show everyday people seeing Spider-Man swing by, which evolved to a simpler sequence that shows casual images of New York City,<ref name="EditorsAotC" /> captured by director Lloyd Lee Choi for the film.<ref name="CrettonAug2026OutNow" /> After the credits, Ned's "Spidey Tracker" app is seen detecting a Spider-Man sighting away from Earth, leading to speculation about which future project it could be teasing.<ref name="PostCredits" /> 

Visual effects were supervised by [[Jerome Chen (visual effects supervisor)|Jerome Chen]] and provided by [[Sony Pictures Imageworks]], [[Framestore]], [[Industrial Light & Magic]] (ILM), [[Rise FX]], [[Cantina Creative]], Soho VFX, and SDFX Studios.<ref name="ArtofVFX" />

== Marketing ==
Cretton discussed the film at Sony's [[CinemaCon]] panel in March 2025, where Holland appeared via a video message to announce the title.<ref name="BrandNewDay" /> Commentators noted the appropriateness of the title, considering the "Brand New Day" comic book storyline explores a reset for Spider-Man following the events of the 2007–2008 "[[One More Day (comics)|One More Day]]" storyline. There was speculation about what impact the events of ''Doomsday'' would have on the film,<ref name="BrandNewDayPolygon" /><ref name="BrandNewDayCB" /><ref name="BrandNewDaySR" /> and whether specific elements from "Brand New Day" would be adapted such as having Parker make a deal with Mephisto to resurrect Aunt May.<ref name="BrandNewDayCB" /> Kevin Erdmann of ''[[Screen Rant]]'' said the "One More Day" and "Brand New Day" storylines were controversial, in part because they reverted Spider-Man's character growth and relationships. He felt the film could follow a similar storyline without undoing the character growth.<ref name="BrandNewDaySR" />

On August 1, 2025—known as "Spider-Man Day"—Sony released an eight-second teaser showing glimpses of the new Spider-Man costume, featuring raised webbing similar to Maguire's suits, and the same faint hexagonal design on the red-and-blue fabric from Holland's previous suits.<ref name="CostumeTeaser" /><ref name="CostumeTeaserPolygon" /><ref name="CostumeTeaserGizmodo" /> A 22-second teaser giving a full view of Holland in costume, directed by his brother Harry, was released the next day.<ref name="CostumeTeaser2Gizmodo" /><ref name="CostumeTeaserDirector" /> Justin Carter at ''[[Gizmodo]]'' said the biggest change other than the raised webbing was the larger spider logo on the chest. Carter also noted the use of Giacchino's MCU Spider-Man theme in the teaser.<ref name="CostumeTeaser2Gizmodo" /> Jordan Moreau at ''Variety'' said the full costume was closer to the comics than Holland's previous costumes,<ref name="CostumeTeaser2Variety" /> while McKinley Franklin of ''The Hollywood Reporter'' said it resembled the suits worn by Maguire and Garfield.<ref name="CostumeTHR" /> On August 10, Sony released a behind-the-scenes video recapping the first day of filming. ''Variety''{{'s}} Jack Dunn felt Sony and Marvel had been generous with the amount of looks at the film so far.<ref name="HollandDayOne" />

The first trailer was released on March 18, 2026.<ref name="TrailerTHR" /> Sony partnered with fans and [[Influencer|content creators]] to release short clips of the trailer on the previous day.<ref name="TrailerClipsTF" /> Tom Bacon at ''[[ComicBook.com]]'' compared this release strategy to a [[Avengers: Doomsday#Marketing|cast announcement livestream]] that Marvel Studios used for ''Doomsday'' the prior year, noting how both were intended to "maximize engagement" throughout the day.<ref name="TrailerClipsCB" /> Rebecah Jacobs at ''[[Men's Journal]]'' and Kaitlyn Booth of ''[[Bleeding Cool]]'' both described the rollout as a [[scavenger hunt]].<ref name="TrailerClipsMJ" /><ref name="TrailerClipsBC" /> Commentators noted that the footage includes the film's homage to the cover of ''Amazing Fantasy'' #15,<ref name="TrailerClipsTF" /><ref name="TrailerClipsCommentary" /> and revealed that Parker's superpowers are mutating to include organic web shooters and black eyes.<ref name="TrailerTF" /><ref name="TrailerEW" /><ref name="TrailerIGN" /> Some believed this was an adaptation of "The Other" or "[[The Six Arms Saga]]" from ''The Amazing Spider-Man'' vol. 1 #100–102 (1971).<ref name="TrailerCommentary" /> Sony said the trailer became the most-viewed ever, with 718.6&nbsp;million views in 24 hours according to market research firm WaveMetrix. It surpassed the record for a film trailer, ''Deadpool & Wolverine''{{'s}} 365&nbsp;million views, in eight hours, and surpassed the overall 24-hour record set by the video game ''[[Grand Theft Auto VI]]'' (2026).<ref name="TrailerViewsTHR" /><ref name="TrailerViewsVariety" /> WaveMetrix further reported that the trailer surpassed 1.1&nbsp;billion views within four days, the first film trailer to cross that threshold.<ref name="TrailerViews1B" />

Rothman and Holland introduced footage from the film at CinemaCon in April 2026.<ref name="CinemaCon2026Variety" /> A second trailer was released on June 17, which gave a better understanding of the film's plot according to Casey Loving at  ''[[TheWrap]]''. He highlighted the return of Banner's uncontrollable "Savage Hulk" persona, and noted the apparent narration from Marisa Tomei's May Parker.<ref name="Trailer2Wrap" /> Commentators discussed the appearance of a mysterious hooded figure in the trailer, whom they believed to be Sink's character.<ref name="Trailer2Commentary" /> WaveMetrix reported that, in one week, the second trailer gathered 590.8&nbsp;million views, becoming the second most-viewed trailer of all time.<ref name="Trailer2Views" /> Sony screened the first 18 minutes of the film at the [[CineEurope]] convention later that month.<ref name="CineEurope2026" /> A final trailer was released on July 21. It largely contained footage from the three previous MCU ''Spider-Man'' films, which Germain Lussier from ''Gizmodo'' said worked well to establish the emotional stakes of ''Brand New Day''.<ref name="Trailer3Gizmodo" /> Bernthal presented a clip from the film at the 2026 [[San Diego Comic-Con]] in late July, showing Spider-Man and the Punisher fighting the Hulk.<ref name="SDCC2026Clip" />

== Release ==
=== Theatrical ===
''Spider-Man: Brand New Day'' premiered at the [[Dolby Theatre]] in [[Hollywood, Los Angeles]], on July 27, 2026.<ref name="WorldPremiere" /> Distributed by [[Sony Pictures Releasing]],<ref name="TrailerWrap" /> the film's international release began on July 29 and it opened in the majority of its markets, including the United Kingdom, over the next two days.<ref name="DeadlineBOTracking" /> In the United States, early screenings occurred for [[Amazon Prime]] members on July 29,<ref name="PrimeEarlyScreenings" /> before it opened on July 31 in 4,487 theaters.<ref name="DeadlineBOFirstWeekend" /> The film was released in premium large formats (PLFs) such as [[ScreenX]], [[RealD 3D]], [[D-Box]], [[Dolby Cinema]], and [[4DX]].<ref name="DeadlineBOFirstWeekend" /><ref name="ReleaseFormats" /> It was also released in [[IMAX]] for select international markets, such as China, Japan, and Korea,<ref name="DeadlineBOTracking" /><ref name="IMAXRelease" /> but not in other markets where ''The Odyssey'' had exclusive use.<ref name="ReleaseFormats" /> ''Brand New Day'' was made available in IMAX globally starting August 6, sharing digital screens with ''The Odyssey'' while that film retained exclusive use of [[70 mm film#IMAX|IMAX's 70 mm]] theaters.<ref name="IMAXGlobal" />

''Brand New Day'' is part of [[Marvel Cinematic Universe: Phase Six|Phase Six]] of the MCU.<ref name="PhaseSix" /> It was previously scheduled for release on July 24, one week after ''The Odyssey''{{'s}} debut, but it was delayed by a week to avoid contention with that film.<ref name="Jul2026DateDelay" /><ref name="Jul2026DateDelay2" /> Disney had intended to release an unspecified Marvel Studios film on July 24, but removed this from the company's release schedule in August 2024 which allowed Sony to schedule ''Brand New Day'' then.<ref name="GizmodoDisneyAug2024Dates" />

=== Home media ===
In April 2021, Sony signed deals with Netflix and Disney for the rights to their 2022 to 2026 film slate, following the films' theatrical and home media windows.<ref name="Netflix" /><ref name="DisneyStreamingDeadline" /> Netflix signed for exclusive "pay 1 window" streaming rights, which is typically an 18-month window and included future ''Spider-Man'' films following ''No Way Home''.<ref name="Netflix" /> Disney signed for "pay 2 window" rights for the films, which would be streamed on Disney+ and [[Hulu]] as well as broadcast on Disney's linear television networks.<ref name="DisneyStreamingDeadline" /><ref name="DisneyStreamingTHR" /> In January 2026, Sony and Netflix announced a new agreement giving Netflix "pay 1 window" rights globally, an increase from just the United States, Germany, and Southeast Asia in the previous deal. The new agreement would begin later in 2026 as territory rights become available, before being fully in effect in early 2029.<ref name="NetflixGlobal" />

== Reception ==
=== Box office ===
{{As of|2026|9|4|df=US}}, ''Spider-Man: Brand New Day'' has grossed $904&nbsp;million in the United States and Canada, and $1.442&nbsp;billion in other territories, for a worldwide total of $2.345&nbsp;billion,<ref name="BOM" /><ref name="NUM" /> making it the [[2026 in film#Highest-grossing films|highest-grossing film of 2026]],<ref name="HighestBOTuesday" /> and the [[List of highest-grossing films|third-highest grossing film of all time]].<ref>{{Cite web |title=Top Lifetime Grosses |url=https://www.boxofficemojo.com/chart/ww_top_lifetime_gross/ |url-status=live |archive-url=https://web.archive.org/web/20260903165221/https://www.boxofficemojo.com/chart/ww_top_lifetime_gross/ |archive-date=September 3, 2026 |access-date=September 3, 2026 |website=[[Box Office Mojo]]}}</ref> Its $932&nbsp;million opening weekend was the [[List of highest-grossing openings for films#Biggest worldwide openings on record|second-best of all time]] behind ''[[Avengers: Endgame#Box office|Avengers: Endgame]]'' (2019).<ref name="FirstWeekendRecordsDeadline" /> On August 3, it became the [[List of fastest-grossing films#Fastest to $1 billion|second-fastest film]] to gross $1&nbsp;billion, doing so in six days.<ref name="BillionSixDaysDeadline" /> On August 16, it became the second-fastest film to gross $2&nbsp;billion and the [[Spider-Man in film#Box office|first ''Spider-Man'' film to do so]].<ref name="2BillionTHR" />

The film had the best first-day ticket pre-sales in five years since ''No Way Home'' in 2021, which had an estimated $78&nbsp;million in pre-sales.<ref name="BestUSTicketPresales" /> Total pre-sales were $150&nbsp;million, a record.<ref name="FirstWeekendRecordsDeadline" /> It had the [[List of highest-grossing openings for films#Biggest openings in the United States and Canada|highest opening weekend gross of all time]] at $360&nbsp;million, surpassing ''Endgame'' ($357.1&nbsp;million). The opening day gross (including previews) of $169.8&nbsp;million also surpassed ''Endgame''{{'s}} record ($157.4&nbsp;million). The Saturday and Sunday grosses of $101.5&nbsp;million and $88.7&nbsp;million, respectively, were both second best all-time to ''Endgame'' ($109.2&nbsp;million and $90.3&nbsp;million).<ref name="DeadlineBOFirstWeekend" /> Pre-release estimates had the film grossing $260–280&nbsp;million in its opening weekend, with Sony estimating a $195&nbsp;million opening.<ref name="VarietyBOTracking" /><ref name="DeadlineBOTracking" /> Its fourth and fifth days of release saw the highest Monday and Tuesday grosses of any film at $47&nbsp;million and $42&nbsp;million, respectively,<ref name="BillionSixDaysVariety" /><ref name="HighestBOTuesday" /> and its Wednesday gross of $32.7&nbsp;million was the second-best behind ''[[Star Wars: The Force Awakens]]'' (2015).<ref name="Deadline500MUS" /> The second weekend gross of $144&nbsp;million was the third-biggest behind ''Endgame'' ($147&nbsp;million) and ''The Force Awakens'' ($149&nbsp;million), and the highest-grossing second weekend for a Sony film,<ref name="OtherTerritories1Billion" /><ref name="SecondWeekendBO" /> while its third weekend gross of $71&nbsp;million was the second highest behind ''The Force Awakens'' ($90.2&nbsp;million).<ref name="DeadlineBOThirdWeekend" /> The film remained top of the box office through its sixth weekend.<ref name="DeadlineBOFourthWeekend" /><ref name="DeadlineBOFifthWeekend" /><ref name="DeadlineBOSixthWeekend" /> It became the fastest film to reach cumulative grosses through $900&nbsp;million.<ref name="FastestBOM" /><ref name="Deadline500MUS" /><ref name="FastestDeadline" /> It is the highest grossing film of 2026,<ref>{{Cite web |title=2026 Top-Grossing Movies |archive-date=August 5, 2026 |access-date=August 6, 2026 |website=[[The Numbers (website)|The Numbers]]}}</ref> and second-highest grossing film ever, after ''The Force Awakens''.<ref ==== Other territories ====
The film grossed $573&nbsp;million its opening weekend,<ref name="VarietyBOFirstWeekend" /> surpassing pre-release estimates of $275–300&nbsp;million.<ref name="VarietyBOTracking" /><ref name="DeadlineBOTracking" /> It opened as the number one film in 66 [[box office market]]s,<ref name="BillionSixDaysDeadline" /> and had the highest-grossing opening weekend all-time in at least 11 countries, including France ($26.6&nbsp;million), Brazil ($23.8&nbsp;million), Spain ($15.6&nbsp;million), and Peru ($8.6&nbsp;million).<ref name="FirstWeekendRecordsDeadline" /> In its second weekend, it remained the top film in most of its 67 markets. === Critical response ===
[[File:TomHolland-byPhilipRomano.jpg|thumb|upright=0.75|alt=Refer to caption|[[Tom Holland]] (pictured in July 2026) received praise for his performance.]]
<!-- Local consensus for MCU articles is to NOT use aggregate templates! Do NOT convert the prose here to them! -->
''Brand New Day'' received largely positive reviews from critics, with praise for Holland's performance, the action sequences, and the more mature, character-driven story compared to the previous MCU ''Spider-Man'' films.<ref name="RTReviewRoundup" /><ref name="BBCReviewRoundup" /><ref name="THRSuccess5Reasons" /><ref name="EWReviewRoundup" /> On review aggregator [[Rotten Tomatoes]], 89% of 428 critics gave a positive review and the average of rated reviews was 7.6/10. The critics consensus reads, "Spinning a web of spectacular set pieces together with mature emotional stakes and Tom Holland's soulful performance, ''Brand New Day'' is a promising reset that portends Spidey will remain sticky on the big screen for years to come."<ref>{{Cite Rotten Tomatoes |id=spider_man_brand_new_day |type=m |title=Spider-Man: Brand New Day |access-date=September 4, 2026}}{{cbignore}}</ref> [[Metacritic]], which uses a [[weighted average]], assigned a score of 66 out of 100 based on 56 critics, indicating "generally favorable" reviews.<ref>{{Cite Metacritic |id=spider-man-brand-new-day |type=movie |title=Spider-Man: Brand New Day |access-date=August 7, 2026}}{{cbignore}}</ref> Audiences polled by [[CinemaScore]] gave the film an average grade of "A" on an A+ to F scale, while 88% of those surveyed by [[PostTrak]] said they would definitely recommend the film.<ref name="DeadlineBOFirstWeekend" />

Pete Hammond for ''[[Deadline Hollywood]]'' called ''Brand New Day'' a "dark and thrilling new chapter" for the franchise, praising Holland's performance, Cretton's direction, and the film's balance of character drama and superhero action.<ref name="DeadlineReview" /> [[Owen Gleiberman]] at ''Variety'' praised the film for its action, villain, and Holland's performance, but described the film as "arduous", "clunkily episodic", and "half an hour too long", arguing that it is more self-serious than emotionally involving.<ref name="VarietyReview" /> [[Peter Bradshaw]] for ''[[The Guardian]]'' praised the ambitious tone, strong performances and emotional focus, particularly highlighting Zendaya and Holland's chemistry. However, he found the film uneven, with some familiar superhero elements limiting its impact.<ref name="TheGuardianReview" /> 

William Bibbiani of ''TheWrap'' praised ''Brand New Day'' as "a fantastic film about Peter Parker" and "one of the highlights of the whole franchise", commending its emotional, character-driven story and the performances of Holland and Bernthal, despite criticizing the villain's plan as illogical.<ref name="TheWrapReview" /> ''[[Empire (magazine)|Empire]]''{{'s}} John Nugent gave the film four out of five stars, praising its grounded, comic-faithful approach and Holland's performance, describing Spider-Man as "still Marvel's ace in the pack", while noting that the second half loses momentum due to unnecessary ties to the wider MCU.<ref name="EmpireReview" /> David Rooney of ''The Hollywood Reporter'' praised Cretton's direction for balancing "adrenaline and feeling", while highlighting "the humanity that has always been Spidey's greatest strength", Holland's "never-better" performance, and the film's emotional focus on Peter, though he criticized occasional pacing issues and plot convolutions.<ref name="THRReview" />

[[David Ehrlich]] at ''[[IndieWire]]'' gave the film a C−, describing it as a "dull and downbeat action sequel" that "causes more problems than it cures", criticizing its pacing, its reliance on MCU setup, and its failure to meaningfully advance Peter's story, although he praised Holland's performance and considered Cretton "a more sure-footed stylist" than Watts.<ref name="IndieWireReview" /> David Fear for ''[[Rolling Stone]]'' criticized the film for relying too heavily on fan service, MCU crossovers and franchise setup at the expense of Spider-Man's story, arguing that Peter risks becoming "a supporting player in his own movie", while praising Holland's performance and the emotional scenes between Peter and MJ.<ref name="RollingStoneReview" />

Pascal stated in November 2021 that ''Brand New Day'' was planned to be the first film in a new ''Spider-Man'' trilogy starring Holland.<ref name="PascalNov2021" /> In early 2026, Holland revisited his previous comments about not wanting to play Spider-Man past the age of 30 and hoping to pass the mantle to another actor. Since the latter had not yet happened by his 30th birthday, Holland said he may need to "change the quote to 37" and added that "playing Spider-Man has been the joy of my life... I'll do it for as long as they'll have me."<ref name="HollandJun2026GQ" /> In July, Holland said he had a clear vision for how he wanted to pass the mantle.<ref name="HollandJul2026Variety" /> Feige said Miles Morales would be introduced in live-action following the release of the animated film ''[[Spider-Man: Beyond the Spider-Verse]]'' (2027), and he had discussed ideas for the next five live-action ''Spider-Man'' films with Pascal.<ref name="FeigeShanghaiMediaJul2026" /> The latter hoped Cretton would be involved in future films,<ref name="CrettonJul2026CB" /> and Cretton said he was open to directing another.<ref name="CrettonAug2026Wrap" /> Following ''Brand New Day''{{'s}} release, Rothman said there had not yet been concrete discussions about Holland returning for a fifth film,<ref name="RothmanAug2026" /> while Feige said Holland and Zendaya would be taking a break before any new plans were confirmed.<ref name="FeigeAug2026" />
